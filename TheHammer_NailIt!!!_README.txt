The start scene is named "prototype" it is in the Assests folder
To control the game you have to have a controller movement is based on the left analog stick, camera movement is on the right analog stick, and jump is the bottom face button(on xbox controller would be A).
In the current stage you run around looking for collectables and nails, once you collect them, the screen should have "You Win".
The collectables and nials are all over the level there are 30 collectables and 16 nails. There is also a red breakable wall with collectabels and a nial behind it.
To break the wall run into it.
Currently you should not go under moving platforms because they can shove you through the world. Camera clips through terrain
